# Focus Pet Website Project Context

## Overview

Focus Pet is a cozy desktop focus companion for Windows.

It combines a distraction-free focus timer with a relaxing virtual pet that quietly grows alongside the user's productivity.

The goal is not to gamify productivity through pressure.

Instead, Focus Pet makes focusing feel comforting by rewarding consistency with cozy customization and gradual progression.

The pet is designed to feel like a companion, not a responsibility.

---

# Core Philosophy

The pet should never feel like a chore.

Focus Pet intentionally avoids:

- Hunger systems
- Friendship meters
- Daily streak punishment
- Tamagotchi mechanics
- Energy depletion
- Notifications demanding attention

The pet exists to celebrate progress, not create guilt.

The player should look forward to returning because they enjoy decorating and spending time with their pet.

---

# Gameplay Loop

The core gameplay loop is extremely simple.

Focus

↓

Pet sleeps peacefully

↓

Complete session

↓

Earn Energy

↓

Buy new items

↓

Decorate your area

↓

Unlock new areas

↓

Repeat

Everything in Focus Pet is designed around this loop.

# Rewards

Players earn:

## Energy

Used to:

- purchase decorations
- unlock new areas

## Strength

Earned through Deep Focus.

Used to purchase premium decorations and special items.

Strength is intentionally much slower to earn.

---

# Focus Pro

Focus Pro is an optional premium upgrade.

It expands the focus experience with additional features while keeping the base experience fully enjoyable.

The website should present Focus Pro positively without making the free version feel limited.

---

# Local First

Focus Pet is designed around a local-first philosophy.

The application works completely offline.

Cloud Sync exists as a convenience rather than a requirement.

The player always owns their save data.

# Website Goals

The website is **not** the application.

Its only goals are:

1. Explain what Focus Pet is.

2. Show why it feels different from other productivity apps.

3. Showcase beautiful screenshots.

4. Encourage visitors to download the Windows application.

5. Explain Focus Pro.

6. Build trust through FAQ, roadmap, and privacy information.

---

# Tone

The website should feel:

- warm
- optimistic
- relaxing
- friendly
- minimal

Avoid:

- corporate language
- productivity buzzwords
- aggressive marketing
- hype

Instead, speak naturally.

"Maximize your productivity through advanced workflow optimization."
